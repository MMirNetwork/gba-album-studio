    About this project

    GBA Album Studio is an open-source project by MMirNetwork.

    This browser edition turns your music and artwork into a playable Game Boy Advance album. MMirNetwork is the creator of this project edition, not the author of every underlying component.

    Built on GSM Player by Damian Yerrick and the album-art fork by Ben Wiley. GSM codec by Jutta Degener and Carsten Bormann, with contributions from the Toast contributors. Their copyright notices and license terms remain applicable.

    Technology, limits & privacy

    Audio and images are processed only in memory on your device. Files are not uploaded, and projects are not permanently saved. Reloading the page clears your album setup.

    • GSM is lossy: output is mono at 18157 Hz, not hi-fi audio.
    • WAV, MP3 and FLAC are decoded by your browser. Use a current version of Chrome, Edge or Firefox. Unsupported variants produce an error message.
    • Up to 150 MiB and 15 minutes per source track, 99 tracks, and a 32 MiB finished ROM. Large files can use substantial memory, especially on mobile devices.
    • GBA text uses ASCII. Accented letters are simplified and unsupported characters replaced. The exact exported name appears beneath each title.
    • On the GBA: A/B pauses, the D-pad changes tracks, L/R seeks, SELECT locks the controls, and holding START shows project and album info.

    Only use music and artwork you have permission to use. Test your exported file in mGBA before using it on real hardware.
